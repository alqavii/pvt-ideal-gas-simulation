# Experiment P1: PVT Properties of an Ideal Gas - Virtual Apparatus

Virtual experimental apparatus for Experiment P1: PVT Properties of an Ideal Gas (MBCE114 / CMB133), Department of Chemical and Biological Engineering, University of Sheffield.

## Overview

A realistic, barebones virtual simulation of the physical apparatus:
- **Vertical Capillary Tube ($d = 2.7\text{ mm}$):** Sealed at the base with trapped dry air column and a mercury plug seal.
- **Centimeter & Millimeter Scale:** 0 to 22 cm ruler running along the capillary.
- **Optical Scale Magnifier (3.6x):** Hairline crosshairs to align and measure the meniscus height ($h$) and mercury plug height ($h_{\text{Hg}}$).
- **Vacuum Hand Pump with Analog Gauge:** Dial calibrated in mbar (0 to -500 mbar), squeeze handle, fine pump/release controls, and vent valve V1.
- **Environmental Immersion:** Ambient Air, Hot Water Bath, and Ice/Water Bath with live immersion thermometers.
- **Realistic Physical Errors:** Contact-angle stick-slip friction on the capillary wall, gauge mechanical backlash, and a "Tap Tube" control to settle the meniscus.
