Laboratory Procedure (MBCE114)

  1. Note the ambient pressure Pamb from the lab barometer.
  2. Measure the height of the mercury seal (hHg) using the scale.
  3. Use the hand pump to generate under-pressure (ΔP, negative gauge reading). Tap the tube to settle the meniscus.
  4. Measure the air column height (h) from the tube bottom (0.0 cm) to the lower meniscus.
  5. Record 10 data points across the pressure range.
  6. Repeat the procedure submerged in hot water and in the ice/water bath.
P = Pamb + ΔP + PHg
PHg = ρHg · g · hHg
V = (π · d² / 4) · h  (d = 2.7 mm)
